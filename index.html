The Firm

A practice built on rigour
and relationships.

Shanti Ved & Associates LLP is a firm of Chartered Accountants based in C-Scheme, Jaipur. We work with businesses, institutions, and individuals on the financial matters that matter most — statutory compliance, sound reporting, and considered advice.

Our approach is deliberately simple: understand the client's affairs thoroughly, apply the law faithfully, and communicate clearly. We aim to be advisers our clients can rely on for the long term.

  • Integrity

    Advice grounded in law and given without compromise.

  • Rigour

    Every engagement handled with method, depth, and documentation.

  • Partnership

    Long-term relationships, built on responsiveness and trust.

What We Do

Services

01

Audit & Assurance

Statutory, internal, and management audits conducted with independence and method — giving stakeholders confidence in reported numbers.

02

Direct Taxation

Income-tax planning, computation, and return filing for businesses and individuals, alongside representation in assessment and appellate proceedings.

03

GST & Indirect Taxes

Registration, periodic compliance, reconciliations, and advisory on the GST law as it applies to your transactions.

04

Corporate Governance & Compliance

Company-law compliance, secretarial coordination, and governance frameworks that keep boards informed and entities in good standing.

Risk Management & Internal Controls

06

Strategic Financial Advisory

Considered counsel on structuring, finance, and significant decisions — grounded in the numbers and the law.
